Frequently asked

Does elbow work across my devices?

Yes. elbow syncs through your personal iCloud account, so your rooms, tasks, and cleaning history stay up to date across your iPhones and iPad, as long as you're signed into the same Apple account with iCloud Drive enabled.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. elbow has no accounts, analytics, or trackers. Everything you enter lives on your device and in your own private iCloud — we can't see it. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

How does household sharing work?

Sharing uses Apple's built-in iCloud sharing. You invite the people you want, and everyone in the shared home sees the same tasks and what's been done. You can stop sharing at any time. Household sharing is a Pro feature.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose elbow to change or cancel. A lifetime purchase is one-time and never renews.

I think I lost some data — can you help?

Because your data lives in your own iCloud rather than on our servers, the first thing to check is that you're signed into the right Apple account with iCloud enabled. If something still looks wrong, email us at hello@byaga.dev and we'll do our best to help.

I have a feature idea or found a bug.

We'd genuinely love to hear it. Drop us a note at hello@byaga.dev — feedback from people using elbow shapes what we build next.
